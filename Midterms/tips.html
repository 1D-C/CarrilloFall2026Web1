One of the first things you need to know is to have patience, just like everything it needs time to learn the necessary skills. While it is quite easy after learning and looking back on it its a bit of a challenge to learn things like double crochet, slip stitch, or crocheting on the front loop.

Now while yes that might seem intimidating its very similar for the first two crocheting stitches I mentioned earlier, and this can all be learned on youtube. Yes youtube, not a joke I started out from there but the videos are eithe obscure or they literally dont help because the hand covers up the work. Again patience but of course there are good videos out there for both types of hand users like:
