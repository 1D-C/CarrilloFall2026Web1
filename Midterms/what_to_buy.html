What to buy



If you truly do want to learn crochet you also got to learn where to get the tools and supplies to even start. If you want to save money you can go towards dollar stores, for both yarn or hooks. The best price I have known is around five dollars for one skein (ball) of yarn. The better the place the priceir it gets so make sure you get your money's worth and have fun with it. There are also different types of yarn to work with so go out and try to find different types to work with.
