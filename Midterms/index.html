Crash Course in Crochet



What is crochet about?

Well its a craft where you generally use one line of yarn to hook and make various sorts of items that can range from simple small items to complex and intricate designs in clothing, amigurumi (basicallly stuffed toys) and accessories like bags, charms, and even book marks if you're feeling creative enough. Its something you can do where ever you go. And if you're skilled enough you can do it without looking, something ive been working on.

Also you can make money off of it if you truly enjoy both making crochet, and selling. This type of skill isnt easy to automate since every pattern out there is unique. And for anyone looking for yarn, who knows you might find it where you didnt even think to look.

Fun facts

Did you know that while crochet and knitting are similar in the fact they both use yarn to make items, crochet is typically done with a single hook while knitting uses two needles, and you can even use pencils instead of needles for knitting if you wanna save your money. Just make sure you wash it well. Also its a bit of a rude comment as about 67% of the time, when people who share about this hobby, they hear most out of times, something along the lines of: "like knitting" or "aren't they the same thing?" So make sure to know the difference as there are other yarn crafts out there like macramé.
